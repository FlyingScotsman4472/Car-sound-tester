What this can and can't do: Free, no adaptor, no subscription, and no ECU connection. The Maserati preset is a generated approximation rather than a real recorded F136. Manual and demo modes work without GPS. GPS mode follows road speed and acceleration, not actual engine revs. Exact RPM matching needs vehicle telemetry such as an OBD2 adaptor.

NSW P-platers: Current NSW rules prohibit P1/P2 drivers from using any mobile phone function while driving, including Bluetooth audio. If you hold a P licence, use this only while safely parked or when you are a passenger. Never mask traffic, horns or emergency vehicles.
